# Unofficial project and unresolved launch review

RankdLab is independent and unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by Psyonix or Epic Games. Rocket League and related marks belong to
their respective owners. The interface includes this disclaimer.

The arena, car meshes, diagrams and UI in this repository are original geometric
work. No proprietary car models, textures, game logos, sounds or arena assets
are bundled. The upstream replay-player assets are not copied into the app.

Before commercial launch, obtain qualified review of:

- Game-content, trademark/nominative-use and replay-data permissions, including
  any relevant game or platform agreements and distribution restrictions.
- Permission to retain, process and share replay data that may identify other
  players, user notices, data rights and privacy/retention/deletion requirements.
- Any licence/permission restrictions attached to replay samples, coaching
  labels, expert feedback, future training datasets and exported reports.
- Third-party licences, required notices, modifications and binary distribution
  obligations across the exact release dependency graph and container images.
- Claims about coaching quality, model evaluation and hypothetical alternatives.
  SSL-level coaching is not claimed or validated.

`subtr-actor-py` and the inspected `@rlrml/player` / `@rlrml/subtr-actor` packages
declare MIT licences; that library licence alone does not establish commercial
rights to game content or all replay files. React, Three.js, Vite, Tailwind,
FastAPI, SQLAlchemy and other dependencies retain their own licences. Preserve
their notices when distributing a release, and generate a release SBOM/licence
report from the lockfiles. No blanket commercial clearance is asserted here.

The optional compatibility fixtures are downloaded from a pinned public upstream
commit into ignored `.research/`, with provenance/checksums recorded in the fetch
script. They are used only to test parsing/rendering, not redistributed as product
content or passed off as coaching labels. The user's original learning files
remain untouched. Do not add private player replays to Git.

There is no paid subscription, checkout or entitlement system. Do not enable
monetisation until the commercial permissions and legal requirements have been
reviewed. This document records open questions; it is not a legal conclusion.
